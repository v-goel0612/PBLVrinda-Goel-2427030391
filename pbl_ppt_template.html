Honeypot Trap System

Detecting malicious activities through intelligent deception—a dynamic honeypot system designed to attract attackers, record their behavior, and strengthen cybersecurity defense mechanisms.

Problem Statement

Modern security systems struggle to detect unknown threats and zero-day attacks because they rely on known signatures. Traditional monitoring fails to provide insight into attacker behavior and often detects intrusions only after damage occurs. This reactive approach is resource-intensive and ineffective. Our honeypot system overcomes these challenges by creating a decoy environment that attracts attackers, enabling early detection, protecting real assets, and providing valuable intelligence on evolving attack techniques.

Literature Review

Existing honeypot systems like Honeyd, Dionaea, Cowrie, Kippo, and Glastopf provide valuable threat detection capabilities. These tools simulate network services, trap malware, and monitor unauthorized access. Our innovation builds upon this foundation with enhanced realism and adaptability.

Innovation & Gap

We enhance honeypot effectiveness through realistic environments, dynamic deployment, customized configurations, and adaptive behavior. Our system simulates connected devices with consistent responses and simulated user activity, making it significantly harder to detect and more effective at capturing meaningful security intelligence.

System Methodology

Dataset & Input

Network traffic logs, attack signatures, system behavior patterns, and real-time threat intelligence feeds processed through advanced analytics.

Model & Architecture

Multi-tier honeypot infrastructure with SSH/HTTP traps, dynamic response systems, behavior analysis engines, and centralized logging mechanisms.
